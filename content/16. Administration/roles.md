### Roles
```
Admin can update system roles and permissions.
These are permissions or responsibilities that are assigned to the users within the system. 
```
![](/images/roole.png)

***Step:one***
 
 In the role listing , you'll see a  button on thre right side **add role** and **add permission**.

  **Add role**  
 This is where you fill the form on the role you were assigned to.

 **Add permission**


***Step two***
On the right there are two buttons:

 In the **Assign menus/sun menus** the system takes you to a form where you can assign which main modules and menus this role is allowed to see. 

 In the **Custom menus** this is the blue button where it allows the administrator to define specific actions each role is authorized to perform within unit master.
