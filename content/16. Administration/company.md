### Company
```
Updating of company info, and logo.
```
![](/images/detail.png)

On the right side there are buttons:
 
The **Edit button** is used to edit the company details.It has the dark blue background.
The **View button** is used to check the company details. It has the blue button.
The **Plus button** is used to display the bank details for each company, as every company has its own bank. It as a yellow color.

When you open the **plus yellow button** it displays the bank company details.
![](/images/ACB.png)

On the right side of the page there's a button **Add bank details** Where you add the company bank account details.
Then you **save** the details.
