###	Direct Purchase

***Step One***
```
Go to Transactions menu then click on the Direct Purchase sub-menu. This allows a user to make a direct purchase to a member’s account.
```
***Step Two***
```
The user begins by searching for a member using the member number and then selecting the member’s details from a dropdown menu.
```
![](/images/ser.png)

***Step Three***

The user then populates the required fields. I.e., account number, transaction date, mode of payment, amount, reference number, source of funds and additional information regarding the transaction.

![](/images/summ.png)

After filling all details you now **submit** the form.
