### Withdrawal schedule

```
The user clicks on the Schedules menu then click the withdrawal schedule submodule. User has an option of scheduling per fund or all funds, can also schedule all withdrawals of select on the available options. A table containing scheduled batches is available for the user to view and download the payout files.
```

The **View mobile schedule** button  allows you to see the schedule of transactions made through mobile channels, such as planned or recurring mobile payments, including their dates, amounts, and statuses.


The **View Bank Schedule**  allows you to view the schedule of transactions processed through the bank, showing planned or recurring bank payments, their timelines, amounts, and current status.
