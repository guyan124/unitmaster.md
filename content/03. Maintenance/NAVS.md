### ***Step Two***

![](/images/Navs.png)

 The **Confirmed button** indicates that a record or action has been verified and approved.
 The **Edit button** allows authorized users to modify the details of the record if changes or corrections are needed. The **Delete button** permanently removes the record from the system when it is no longer required, usually after a confirmation step.

 ![](/images/nav1.png)

 On the right side theres a button called **Add Rate Based Nav** and **Add Nav**.
 The **rate-based Net Asset Value** (NAV) is calculated using a predefined rate or pricing rule and is often applied before or during fund operations to estimate the value of assets.
 
 When you click the **Add Rate Based Nav** It shows the  applicable date to calculalte the nav it self. It is calculated by the system.
 ![](/images/APPLI.png)
 
  In contrast, the **NAV** defined after fund computation is the final and actual value, calculated after all fund activities—such as contributions, withdrawals, expenses, and income—have been fully processed.
 ![](/images/nav2.png)
