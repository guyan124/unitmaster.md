### **Securities/Funds overview**
```
Securities / funds is a collective investment scheme where money from many investors is pooled together to purchase short term, low risk and highly liquid debt instruments.

A professional fund manager invests this pooled money in assets like treasury bills, commercial paper and fixed deposits, and the returns are distributed to the investors, minus a management fee.

This structure provides investors with a way to earn a return on their savings while still having easy access to their cash.
```

 The buttons on the right side: 


The **Edit button** lets authorized users update the details of a fund or security. It ensures that all records remain accurate and current. It has the dark blue color.


The **Delete button** allows users to permanently remove a fund or security entry that is no longer needed. Usually, it asks for confirmation to prevent accidental deletion. It has the red color.


The **View button** displays all the details of a fund or security without allowing any changes. It provides a safe way to review information for auditing or reference purposes. It has the blue color.
